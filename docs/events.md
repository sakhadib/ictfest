**Team Size:** 5-7 members

**Final Match Date:** 25 July, 2026 (final \- onsite)  
**Registration and Team Formation:**

* Any undergraduate university student can register and form a team.  
* Cross-university teams are not allowed. All the members in the team should be from the same university.   
* Each team will consist at least of 5 players and no more than 7 players including substitutes. Each team can have a coach but it’s not mandatory.  
* A player can play for only one team.  
* There are no rank limits, players of any ranks can register.  
* Grand Finale will be in LAN and the teams must be present with the full lineup to participate.

**Format:** The tournament consists of 32 teams. The first 2 rounds will be a direct knockout. The entire tournament will be played in a double elimination format, i.e, there will be an upper bracket and a lower bracket. If someone loses in the upper bracket, they will be sent to the lower bracket for another chance. Those who lose in the lower bracket will be eliminated from the tournament entirely.  
**Guidelines:** For LAN events, it's imperative that each team member carries their university student ID card for verification purposes. Ensure your eligibility by bringing your ID to the event. All teams participating in the LAN event must bring their own keyboard, mouse, headset, and mousepad.
